Hippocampus Limbic System

Location: Deep Medial Temporal Lobe

AP Mnemonic: "If you saw a Hippo on Campus, you'd REMEMBER it!"
Primary Function & Role

Consolidates short-term memories into long-term explicit (declarative) memories. Essential for spatial navigation and episodic memory processing.

Damage / Lesion Impact (AP Clinical)

Anterograde Amnesia: Inability to form new explicit long-term memories (e.g. Patient H.M.). Procedural/implicit memory remains intact.

AP Exam Key Terms & Neurotransmitters
• Explicit / Declarative Memory
• Acetylcholine (ACh) & Alzheimer's
• Anterograde vs. Retrograde Amnesia

2. Famous AP Psychology Case Studies

• Phineas Gage: Prefrontal cortex tamping iron injury (personality shift, impulse control).
• Patient H.M. (Henry Molaison): Bilateral hippocampal removal (anterograde amnesia).
• Louis Victor Leborgne ("Tan"): Broca's area lesion (expressive aphasia).
• Split-Brain Patients (Sperry & Gazzaniga): Corpus callosum severed to treat epilepsy; visual field/hemispheric lateralization experiments.
