Taylor & Maclaurin Series Guide

AP Calculus BC Core Concepts

1. Taylor & Maclaurin Definitions

The Taylor series for $f(x)$ centered at $x = c$ is $P(x) = \sum_{k=0}^{\infty} \frac{f^{(k)}(c)}{k!} (x-c)^k$. When $c = 0$, it is referred to as a Maclaurin Series.

2. Radius of Convergence (R)

Functions like $e^x$, $\sin(x)$, and $\cos(x)$ converge for all real numbers ($R = \infty$). Geometric-type series like $\frac{1}{1-x}$, $\ln(1+x)$, and $\arctan(x)$ have finite radii ($R = 1$) centered at $c=0$, valid only on $(-1, 1)$.

3. Lagrange Error Bound

The remainder error $|R_N(x)| = |f(x) - P_N(x)|$ is bounded by $|R_N(x)| \le \frac{M}{(N+1)!} |x-c|^{N+1}$, where $M$ is the maximum value of $|f^{(N+1)}(t)|$ on the interval between $c$ and $x$.
