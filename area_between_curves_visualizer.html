Area Between Curves Guide

AP Calculus BC Conceptual Derivation

1. dx Mode (Vertical Slices)

Integrate with respect to $x$ when curves are functions $y = f(x)$ and $y = g(x)$. The area is $\int_a^b [\text{Top}(x) - \text{Bottom}(x)]\,dx$. Slices are vertical rectangles of width $\Delta x$.

2. dy Mode (Horizontal Slices)

Integrate with respect to $y$ when curves are expressed as $x = f(y)$ and $x = g(y)$. The area is $\int_c^d [\text{Right}(y) - \text{Left}(y)]\,dy$. Slices are horizontal rectangles of height $\Delta y$.

3. When to Choose dy over dx on the AP Exam

Choose $dy$ if integrating with $dx$ requires splitting the region into multiple integrals (e.g. where the top or bottom function changes formula mid-region), or when functions are naturally defined in terms of $y$.
