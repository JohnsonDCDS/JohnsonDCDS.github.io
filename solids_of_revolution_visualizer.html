Solids of Revolution Guide

AP Calculus BC Teaching Tool

1. Disk Method

Used when rotating a single region bounded by $f(x)$ directly against the axis of revolution. Slice is a solid circular disk with Area $A(x) = \pi [R(x)]^2$.

2. Washer Method

Used when rotating the region between two functions $f(x)$ and $g(x)$ around an axis. Slice is a circular washer with outer radius $R(x)$ and inner radius $r(x)$. Area $A(x) = \pi ([R(x)]^2 - [r(x)]^2)$.

3. Cylindrical Shell Method

Used when slices are parallel to the axis of revolution (e.g., vertical slices rotated around a vertical axis $x=h$). Surface area of shell $A(x) = 2\pi \cdot \text{Radius}(x) \cdot \text{Height}(x)$.

4. Controls & Shortcuts

• Use the Revolution Angle Slider to cut open the 3D solid and view interior cross-sections.
• Use the Differential Slice Position Slider to observe individual disks/washers/shells traveling along $[a, b]$.
• Mouse controls: Right click/drag to pan, Left click/drag to rotate 3D camera.
