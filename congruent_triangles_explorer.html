Why SSA is NOT a Theorem!

SSA (Side-Side-Angle) specifies two side lengths and a non-included angle. This is also known as the Ambiguous Case.

Because the side opposite the angle acts like a swinging arm on a hinge, it can form TWO non-congruent triangles with exact same given measurements!

💡 Teacher Memory Trick:

SSA spelled backwards is ASS. Remind students: "Don't be an ASS—SSA does NOT prove triangle congruence!" (Except for Right Triangles, where it is called HL).
